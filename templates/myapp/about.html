About Vigilant Sphere

Vigilant Sphere Threat Intelligence & Detection Engine is a local cybersecurity scanner for learning, auditing, and reporting. It combines system, process, startup, network, file, threat-intelligence style, MITRE, response, and reporting modules into a single SOC workflow.

Its goal is to help users understand why an endpoint or file appears risky, what evidence supports the detection, and which remediation steps a SOC analyst would consider next.

Detection

EDR, NDR, file threat analysis, user behavior analytics, and local IOC-style checks.

Mapping

MITRE ATT&CK hints connect findings to analyst-friendly tactics and techniques.

Response

Reports include quarantine, block, isolate, rotate, and escalation recommendations.
