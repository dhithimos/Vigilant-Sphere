About Vigilant Sphere

Local security analysis and evidence management.

Security, intelligence and response

Analyze URLs and file structure, evaluate bounded process snapshots, correlate reviewed indicators, inspect available wireless metadata, and manage incident response.

Each scanner has its own result contract. Findings include observations and limitations; risk scores help prioritize review and do not represent detection guarantees.

Core workflows use Django, SQLite and local analysis. Optional reputation sources depend on configuration and availability. No source being unavailable is treated as a clean result.

ATT&CK mappings refer to the bundled dataset and require supporting evidence. Response playbooks track human actions; they do not execute destructive remediation.
